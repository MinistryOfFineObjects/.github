# Ministry of Fine Objects

**MOFO** is a trade marketplace for contract and residential furniture, lighting,
rugs and decor. Interior designers, architects and businesses buy at trade prices.

## On GitHub

- **[mofo-mcp](https://github.com/MinistryOfFineObjects/mofo-mcp)** explains how to
  connect your AI assistant to MOFO. Your assistant can search the catalogue, read
  product specs and get your trade prices.
- **[mofo-deploys](https://github.com/MinistryOfFineObjects/mofo-deploys)** is a
  public log of every production deploy of MOFO since July 2026. Each deploy has
  a dated entry.
